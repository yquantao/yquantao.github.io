Quantao Yang

yquantao at gmail dot com

I am a postdoctoral researcher at KTH Royal Institute of Technology, working with Prof. Olov Andersson and Prof. Danica Kragic. I am also a founding member of Qualia Robotics. Previously, I worked as a research scientist at ABB Corporate Research in Sweden.

I completed my PhD in Computer Science at Örebro University, advised by Prof. Todor Stoyanov and Prof. Johannes A. Stork. I was also a visiting PhD student at UT Austin, advised by Prof. Yuke Zhu.

I am interested in developing intelligent robotic agents capable of learning and executing tasks across diverse real-world environments.
