About

I’m a postdoctoral researcher at KTH Royal Institute of Technology, working with Prof. Olov Andersson and Prof. Danica Kragic. My research focuses on robot learning and manipulation.

I completed my PhD in Computer Science at Örebro University, advised by Prof. Todor Stoyanov and Prof. Johannes A. Stork. I was also a visiting PhD student at UT Austin, advised by Prof. Yuke Zhu.
